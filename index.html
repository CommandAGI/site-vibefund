How it works

Four steps, and none of them waits for a business day.

01

Open an account

Sign in with a CommandAGI account and an ID check. The demo needs neither; it runs with demo cash.

02

Fund with USDC

Deposit USDC from any chain we settle on. The balance is available the moment the transfer lands.

03

Take shares

Pick a company, set a quantity, and settle at the listed price. Fractions are allowed down to 1/100 of a share.

04

Sell when you like

Post your shares back to the book. Settlement is instant and the USDC is yours to withdraw.

Questions

The short answers. A real one is a mail away.

Is this real trading?

No. This first version is a demo: the twelve companies and their prices are sample data written into the page, and the trades change numbers in your browser's storage. The market for real shares, with real settlement, opens when the owner launches it.

Who can invest?

Accredited investors in the jurisdictions vibeFund is registered in, and nobody else until that changes. The demo asks for nothing, so treat it as a picture of the product, not as an account.

Where does the money sit?

USDC in custody with the licensed custodian, in an account in your name. vibeFund does not lend it, stake it or hold it on its own book.

What does it cost?

No deposit or withdrawal fee and 0.20% per trade, both shown on the ticket before you confirm. The owner sets the final schedule before launch.

How does a startup list?

The founder sets a raise, a pre-money valuation and the shares outstanding. vibeFund reviews the company and its filings, then the round opens at one price per share. Write to hello@vibefund.pro.
